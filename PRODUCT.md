# FORMA: product brief

**What it is.** A generator of imaginary architecture driven by readable rules, and a studio to explore it. A Java engine grows each design from a preset, a seed and parameters; the web app shows it as a lit diorama and opens up every step that made it.

**Who it is for.** People who enjoy procedural generation and architecture: technical artists, game and level designers, students of generative design, and the curious visitor who wants to see how rules become a building. They arrive either from a link to the exhibition page (a few minutes, on a laptop or phone) or to work in the studio (longer sessions at a desk, changing parameters, editing rules, comparing refinements, exporting).

**What they must be able to do.**
1. See something beautiful immediately, and understand it was generated, not modelled.
2. Change a world and generate again, with visible progress and the ability to cancel.
3. Understand how it grew: replay the recorded stages, read the rules and see where each one applied.
4. Trust the result: see which constraints passed, which are heuristics, and what is only presentation.
5. Keep and share: reproducible seeds and configs, project files, PNG and GLB exports.

**How it should feel.** An exhibition of models at night: quiet, precise, warm light on paper and stone. Confident, not playful; technical honesty is part of the tone (an illusion is called an illusion, a heuristic a heuristic).

**What it is not.** Not a CAD or BIM tool, not structural analysis, not a prompt-to-image toy. Every image is a render of geometry the engine produced.

**Voice.** Plain, specific sentences. No marketing superlatives, no em dashes, no eyebrow labels. Numbers shown are measured or computed, never decorative.
